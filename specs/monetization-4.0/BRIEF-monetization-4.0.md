### CLIENT MODEL

Jobzee is free for occasional clients.

CLIENT FREE:
- 0 RON.
- 3 published service requests per calendar month.
- No per-job commission.
- Jobzee Talk and marketplace browsing are free.
- Direct QR requests count toward the same monthly quota.
- Draft conversations do not consume quota.

EXTRA REQUEST:
- Proposed 5 RON per additional published request.

CLIENT PLUS:
- Proposed 19 RON/month.
- Up to 15 published requests per calendar month.
- Optional subscription.
- No per-job matching commission.

URGENT BOOST:
- Proposed 9 RON for 24 hours.
- Optional promotion for an urgent request.
- Urgency can still be declared for free.
- Promotion never guarantees a response or completed job.

### JOBZER MODEL

Use one unified success fee for all eligible Jobzers:

- Individual.
- PFA.
- SRL.

Proposed fee:
- 10% of the accepted offer value.
- Minimum 5 RON.
- Maximum 100 RON.
- No fee for browsing or submitting offers.
- Same commission for marketplace and QR-originated requests.

INDIVIDUAL:
- Jobzer pays the success fee through Jobzee when the match is accepted.
- Direct contact unlocks after verified fee payment.
- The actual service payment occurs directly between Client and Jobzer.
- Jobzee does not process the service payment.

PRO:
- Client pays the accepted service amount through the authorized payment provider.
- The provider allocates the contractual 10% fee to Jobzee.
- The remaining amount is transferred to the professional connected account.
- Model payment-provider costs, VAT, invoicing, refunds, disputes and payout status separately.
- Do not call the system legal escrow unless the provider explicitly supports it.
