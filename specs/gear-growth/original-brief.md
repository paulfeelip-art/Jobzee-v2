## 1. BUSINESS CONCEPT

Jobzee Gear & Growth helps Jobzers:

- Equip themselves for professional work.
- Buy personalized toolkits.
- Order individual tools and accessories.
- Build a professional identity.
- Order branded clothing.
- Create and order business cards.
- Create and order flyers and promotional materials.
- Promote their Jobzee profile using QR codes.
- Track their orders.
- Develop their professional presence.

The primary business model is **made-to-order commerce with partner fulfillment**.

Jobzee should not hold its own inventory at launch.

All physical products should be ordered from partners, manufactured or assembled when necessary, and shipped to the customer.

## 2. FIVE CORE MODULES

### A. JOBZEE GEAR KITS

Personalized professional kits based on the Jobzer's services.

Examples:

- Assembly Kit.
- Cleaning Kit.
- Moving Kit.
- Tech Support Kit.
- Gardening Kit.
- Painting Kit.
- Event Support Kit.
- General Handyman Kit.

Offer:

- Starter.
- Pro.
- Custom.

Each kit must include a transparent list of products, quantities, technical specifications, prices and availability.

Users can remove products they already own.

A purchase does not constitute professional certification.

### B. GEAR SHOP

Individual products:

- Tools.
- Equipment.
- Consumables.
- Work accessories.
- Protective equipment.
- Replacement items.

Filter products by service specialization.

Recommend relevant products without aggressive upselling.

### C. JOBZEE IDENTITY

Personalized merchandise:

- T-shirts.
- Polo shirts.
- Hoodies.
- Caps.
- Work vests.
- Tool bags.
- ID badges.
- Stickers.
- Vehicle magnets.

Use the official Jobzee brand guidelines.

Allow personalization with the Jobzer's name, specialization and approved public profile information.

Do not create misleading certification badges.

### D. JOBZEE PRINT STUDIO

Provide:

- Business cards.
- Flyers.
- Brochures.
- Promotional stickers.
- QR cards.
- Vehicle branding materials.
- Print-ready digital files.

Generate designs automatically from the Jobzer's approved profile.

Include a permanent QR code linked to the Jobzer's public Jobzee profile.

The QR must support a future service-request entry point.

Offer digital previews before ordering.

Allow download of print-ready files where technically supported.

### E. JOBZER GROWTH

Provide:

- Professional profile completeness.
- Declared equipment inventory.
- Skills and qualifications.
- Portfolio.
- Public profile QR.
- Marketing materials.
- Real QR scan statistics.
- Real profile visits.
- Real requests originating from profile promotion.

Do not invent performance data.

Do not promise additional jobs in exchange for buying products.

## 3. SMART KIT BUILDER

Design a guided configuration experience.

Jobzee asks:

1. What services do you provide?
2. What tools and equipment do you already own?
3. What budget or kit level do you prefer?

Jobzee recommends a configurable kit.

The user may:

- Add products.
- Remove products.
- Upgrade items.
- Choose sizes.
- Choose quantities.
- Add branding.
- Save the configuration.
- Review the final price.
- Order the kit.

Reuse Jobzee's short, semi-structured conversational philosophy.

Avoid lengthy questionnaires.

## 4. MADE-TO-ORDER COMMERCE

Design the complete order lifecycle:

Product discovery → Configuration → Preview → Price and availability confirmation → Checkout → Supplier order → Production or assembly → Shipment → Delivery → After-sales support.

Support:

- Multiple suppliers.
- Split shipments.
- Production lead times.
- Personalized items.
- Stock availability at supplier level.
- Shipping fees.
- Tracking numbers.
- Order cancellation rules.
- Returns and warranties.
- Invoices and VAT.
- Refund handling.
- Customer support.

Never invent availability, delivery dates or supplier integrations.

Clearly distinguish made-to-order from immediately available products.

## 7. CRITICAL PRODUCT RULES

- Buying products must not be required to access marketplace opportunities.
- Buying a kit must not automatically unlock skills or certifications.
- Sponsored placement must be clearly identified.
- Organic matching must remain based on relevant eligibility and suitability.
- Never claim products are in stock unless verified.
- Never claim a shipment has been dispatched without supplier confirmation.
- Do not reveal private contact information through public QR profiles.
- QR-generated service requests must follow Jobzee's approved request, offer and matching process.
- Do not integrate physical commerce payments with the separate matching-fee ledger.
- Keep commerce orders and marketplace matching as separate business domains.
